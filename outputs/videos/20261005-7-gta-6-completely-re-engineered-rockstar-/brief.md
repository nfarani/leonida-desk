## Editorial angle

Every outlet reported that GTA VI has hurricanes. None could say whether they do anything, because Rockstar has not said: Eurogamer asked outright whether wet roads will affect tyres. This short sits in that gap on purpose. It states the three things that are on record, says plainly what is missing, then switches to a clearly labelled what-if and plays one police chase through a storm to show why the answer matters: if weather is systemic rather than cosmetic, it changes the most repeated moment in the series.

## What the viewer sees

- Premise: An ordinary high-speed police chase under a clear sky: the escape every player already knows how to run.
- Escalation: The same chase, the same red car, as a storm arrives. Rain eats the visibility, the street floods, the car slides and a pursuer spins out, the helicopter above cannot hold steady, and the usual way out is under water.
- Payoff: The driver stops at the flooded underpass he always uses and has to pick a route he has never taken. The weather, not the police, ended the plan.
- Visual style: photoreal cinematic car chase, one continuous weather progression from clear sky to hurricane, same hero car throughout
