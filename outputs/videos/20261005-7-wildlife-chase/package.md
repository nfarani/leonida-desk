# GTA VI: the cops might be the least of your problems #shorts

`20261005-7-wildlife-chase` · story #7 · gta-vi · HIGH CONFIDENCE REPORT · status READY_FOR_REVIEW · 9:16 · 34s · treatment: WHAT THIS CHANGES

## Editorial angle

Not 'GTA VI has lots of animals'. The video asks what 170 species and a predator-filled swamp could do to the oldest trick in the series: running from the police into the wilderness. It opens on the moment the trick fails, rewinds through one continuous escape from Vice City into Grassrivers, uses three things that are on record (the species count, Rockstar's own description of the swamp, and its own screenshot of a chase through a gator swamp), and then asks, as a labelled what-if, whether the map itself becomes part of the chase.

## What the viewer sees

- **Visual premise:** One continuous escape in one truck: city, highway, dirt track, swamp. Each cut is further from the police and deeper into something else.
- **Visual escalation:** Neon streets, then leaving the road, then birds scattering, then eyes in the water, then the chase ploughing through gators, then the police giving up, then being alone out there.
- **Visual payoff:** From high above: one pair of headlights stopped in an endless dark swamp, the city glowing on the horizon, and eyes shining in the water all around.

## Editing notes

Frame one is the gator and the headlights: no title, no fade. Then a hard cut back to the city, so the first three shots run swamp, city, road: the viewer has seen where this ends before it starts. From shot 3 onward it is one journey in one truck and the light only gets darker. Let shot 5 (the wake) and shot 7 (the cops giving up) breathe; cut everything else on the narrator's full stops. Shot 8 returns to the opening image. Tags: a source credit for the three facts, WHAT IF for the scenario. No HUD, no wanted stars, no game art.

The sound tells the same story as the pictures: sirens and engine in the city, then gravel, then wings, then water, then almost nothing. The sirens must audibly fade during shot 7 so the quiet lands. One low swell under the last line. The voice is always on top.
