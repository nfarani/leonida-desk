From shot 3 onward it is one journey in one truck and the light only gets darker. Let shot 5 (the wake) and shot 7 (the cops giving up) breathe; cut everything else on the narrator's full stops. Shot 8 returns to the opening image. Tags: a source credit for the three facts, WHAT IF for the scenario. No HUD, no wanted stars, no game art.
