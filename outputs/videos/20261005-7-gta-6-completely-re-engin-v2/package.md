## Editorial angle

The article says the weather was rebuilt. The video asks the only question a player cares about: what happens to a police chase when the hurricane hits? It opens on the consequence, gives the two facts that are on record, names the thing Rockstar has not said, and then plays the chase through the storm as a clearly labelled what-if.

- **Visual payoff:** The opening slide returns in slow motion under the last question, so the end runs straight back into the start.

## Editing notes

Frame one is the slide: no fade-in, no title. The first caption is on screen with the first word. The narrator leads and the cuts follow his full stops. Shots 5 to 8 are the escalation and get faster: one consequence, one cut. Leave a beat of near silence before 'You're cooked'. The last shot is the opening slide slowed down: it lets the question land and loops cleanly into frame one. Tags stay small in the corner: ON RECORD for the two facts, NOT CONFIRMED for the question, WHAT IF for everything we are imagining. No HUD, no wanted stars on screen, nothing that imitates a game interface.
