Konami's Silent Hill revival is not ending with the games already released. Producer Motoi Okamoto told Famitsu: "we're not stopping here; we must think of the second and third waves".[^c5]

## What he said

The detail is thin by design. "We can't share specific details, but we're currently looking at a number of projects."[^c6] The interview ran in Famitsu issue 1965 and reached English-language outlets by way of Game Observer, so every English quote here is a translation.[^c8]

Okamoto also explained why the revival began with several games instead of one. In his words, "one remake is not enough", because a single release might have left people expecting another cancellation.[^c7] PC Gamer adds that he described real difficulty persuading Konami's executives after Hideo Kojima's Silent Hills was cancelled in 2015.[^c12]

## The first wave, and what is already known about the next

| Wave | Game | Status |
|---|---|---|
| First | Silent Hill 2 remake | Released[^c9] |
| First | Silent Hill f | Released[^c9] |
| First | Silent Hill: Townfall | Released last month; 8/10 from IGN, four stars from Eurogamer[^c9][^c10][^c11] |
| Later | Remake of the original Silent Hill, by Bloober Team | Announced in June 2025[^c13] |
| Later | Other projects | Under consideration, not named[^c6] |

## Why it matters

The plan worked the way it was meant to. Konami announced a batch of projects together in 2022, and PC Gamer counts three new entries in as many years after roughly a decade with none.[^c14][^c16] That track record is what makes a vague promise of more worth reporting: the last vague promise was kept.[^c9]

Okamoto's reasoning about cancellation also says something about how the series is being run. The risk he describes is one of trust, and several releases in a row is his answer to it.[^c7]

## What we don't know

No titles, dates or platforms were given for anything beyond the Bloober Team remake.[^c6][^c13] It is also open whether NeoBards Entertainment and Screen Burn Interactive, the studios behind Silent Hill f and Townfall, will make another.[^c15]
