Arc System Works has laid out the whole first year of downloadable characters for Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls: Phoenix Cyclops, Thor (Jane Foster), Venom and Jubilee.[^c9] The first arrives on 2 November and the last is due in summer 2027.[^c10]

## The roadmap

| When | What |
|---|---|
| 2 November 2026 | Phoenix Cyclops, with Classic and Legacy costumes[^c10] |
| Winter 2027 | Thor (Jane Foster)[^c10] |
| Spring 2027 | Venom, with a Klyntar stage[^c10] |
| Summer 2027 | Jubilee[^c10] |

The winter window is the vague one. IGN reads it as meaning before March of next year.[^c16] The announcement came alongside Evo France 2026 and included a first look at Thor in an Asgard setting, without gameplay.[^c15] The reveal artwork is by Marvel artist Peach Momoko.[^c12]

## What it costs

| Option | Price |
|---|---|
| Year 1 Character and Stage Pass | $34.99[^c17] |
| Digital Deluxe Edition (includes the DLC characters) | $84.99[^c18] |
| Single characters or the stage | Sold individually; prices not given in our sources[^c11] |

The game is a four-versus-four tag fighter published by Sony Interactive Entertainment, available on PlayStation 5 and on PC.[^c24][^c19]

## Why Cyclops is DLC

Producer Takeshi Yamanaka has explained both the choice of character and the timing. The narrative team suggested the Phoenix version, he said: "We felt this interpretation would allow us to create fresher visuals and a more exciting fighting style while offering something unexpected for players."[^c13] He also said the team "made the difficult decision to continue working on the character until we felt like we were adding something new", which is why Cyclops comes after launch.[^c14]

## Why it matters

A dated roadmap is a statement of intent, and this game needed one. It reviewed well in August, with a 9/10 from IGN, but the Steam version drew mixed user reviews over technical problems and PlayStation Network requirements.[^c21] IGN, citing SteamDB, says the PC player count struggles to pass 1,000 at a time, and reports that some players have worried about the game's future.[^c22][^c23]

Against that background, four named characters running to summer 2027 tell players the studio expects to be supporting the game for at least that long.[^c10] It does not tell them whether the PC audience comes back.[^c22]

The picks themselves follow a pattern. The base roster already favours less expected versions of familiar heroes, such as Shuri as Black Panther, and Phoenix Cyclops and Jane Foster's Thor continue it.[^c20] Venom and Jubilee are the straightforward ones.[^c9]

## What we don't know

Exact dates for the three 2027 characters, the price of each character bought alone, and whether a second year is planned have not been announced in the sources we read.[^c11] Gameplay for Thor, Venom and Jubilee has not been shown.[^c15]
