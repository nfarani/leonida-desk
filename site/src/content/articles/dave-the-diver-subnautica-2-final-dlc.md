Dave the Diver is ending its run of expansions with a crossover: a Subnautica 2 Content Pack, made with Subnautica studio Unknown Worlds, arriving free of charge in early 2027.[^c5]

## The short version

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What is it? | The game's final DLC pack, themed on Subnautica 2[^c5] |
| Price | Free[^c5] |
| When | Early 2027; no day given[^c5] |
| Where does it take place? | The waters of Proteus[^c6] |
| Platforms | The base game is on PlayStation 5 and 4, Xbox Series and One, Switch 2 and Switch, PC, iOS and Android[^c7] |

## What you do in it

Dave and Bancho travel between dimensions to an unfamiliar sea, where they find new creatures and resources, unlock new recipes and help new characters, with a Leviathan turning up from time to time.[^c6] The reveal trailer shows Dave stepping through a portal to Subnautica 2's ocean moon, and the Leviathan noticing.[^c11]

## What the two studios said

Mintrocket's studio director Jaeho Hwang said players had been asking for this pairing for a long time.[^c9] His case for it: "Both games are set underwater, but Subnautica has such a distinct approach to underwater exploration".[^c8] For Unknown Worlds, creative director Anthony Gallegos said "the expansion is a love letter to both Dave the Diver and the Subnautica universe".[^c10]

## Why it matters

Crossovers are usually sold. This one is free, and it is the last one, which makes it read as a send-off more than a product.[^c5] It is also an easier fit than some of the game's earlier guests, a list that includes Godzilla: one game is about catching what lives in the sea, and the other is about being afraid of it.[^c13][^c6]

## What we don't know

Beyond the trailer, detail is slim.[^c14] There is no release day, no word on length, and no sign of a return visit: nothing has been said about Dave appearing in Subnautica 2, which is still in early access.[^c12]
