Rockstar has named the first six radio stations in Grand Theft Auto VI and promised the series' "biggest and widest-ranging selection of in-game radio yet".[^c9][^c10] Two changes matter as much as the playlist: podcasts arrive for the first time, and the radio no longer stops when you leave the car.[^c21][^c22]

## The six stations so far

| Station | Music | In-game hosts | Reported real-world hosts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cocoteo FM | Modern Latin: bachata, merengue, dembow, reggaeton[^c14] | El Martillo and La Gata[^c14] | Bad Bunny and RaiNao[^c23] |
| Back Country Radio | Country[^c15] | MW and Delta Dawne[^c15] | Morgan Wallen, Lana Del Rey and Jeremy Dufrene[^c23] |
| AfroBank FM | African music, old classics to amapiano and 3-step[^c16] | Burna and Palmsy[^c16] | Burna Boy and DJ Palms Trax[^c23] |
| The Chamber 106.6 | Classic metal from the 1980s onward[^c17] | DJ Kerry and DJ Tom[^c17] | Kerry King and Tom Araya[^c23] |
| Flash FM | Pop[^c18] | Robyn and Alex[^c18] | Robyn and Alex Consani[^c23] |
| Dirty South Classics | Regional rap and hip-hop classics[^c19] | Trick and Trina[^c19] | Trick Daddy and Trina[^c23] |

The station names, genres and in-game host names come from Rockstar's own descriptions, as reproduced by Pure Xbox.[^c14][^c19] The real-world names in the last column are a step removed: Eurogamer gives them on the authority of Rolling Stone, so treat that column as reported.[^c23]

Each station has an audio preview on Rockstar's website, next to six singles from the soundtrack with artists including Yung Lean and Travis Scott.[^c11] Pure Xbox picked out Bad Bunny, David Allan Coe, Black Sabbath, Pantera, Slayer, Charli xcx, MGMT and DJ Khaled in the clips.[^c20]

## What is new: podcasts and headphones

Rockstar's statement says the game "introduces a series of on-demand podcasts for Jason and Lucia to experience beyond Leonida's terrestrial radio".[^c21] Both the podcasts and the stations are "available to listen to in vehicles and on foot through newly introduced headphones and earbuds connected to their smartphones".[^c22]

## Small details worth noticing

The descriptions double as a map. Back Country Radio broadcasts to the Grassrivers and Port Gellhorn, and has a roving reporter, Mud Dog Mike, who checks in from an airboat.[^c15] Flash FM is called a Vice City institution.[^c18]

## Why it matters

Listening on foot is new for the series, as Eurogamer points out.[^c22] Putting the radio on a phone changes when a player hears it: the walk between a parked car and a mission, a stretch on foot through a swamp, any moment that would otherwise be silent.[^c22]

Podcasts being on demand is the more interesting word. A radio station plays what it plays; something on demand is chosen, which suggests listening that a player can start and return to rather than catch by chance.[^c21] How many there are, how long they run and who voices them has not been said.

## What we don't know

The six are a first batch, with more stations to be shared later.[^c12] Rockstar's wording mentions classic rock and talk radio, and neither appears among the six, so at least those two formats look likely to follow.[^c13][^c10] Full track lists have not been published, and nothing here says whether players can bring their own music.

GTA VI is due on 19 November.[^c24] We will update this guide as more stations are named.
