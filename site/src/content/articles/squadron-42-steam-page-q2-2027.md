Squadron 42, the single-player game set in the Star Citizen universe, now has a Steam page, a new trailer and a full cast list.[^c15] The page gives a release date of Q2 2027.[^c17]

## What is on the record now

| Detail | What the sources say |
|---|---|
| Announced | 10 October[^c16] |
| Release | Q2 2027, as listed on Steam[^c17] |
| Install size | 150GB[^c17] |
| System requirements | Published; no mention of ray-tracing cards or DirectX 12[^c18] |
| Setting | The year 2945; you are a rookie in the UEE Navy aboard a capital ship[^c22] |
| Technology | StarEngine, the studio's own[^c22] |
| Player characters | Ian Duncan or Alix Wilton Regan[^c24] |

The supporting cast is the headline act: Mark Hamill, Gary Oldman, Gillian Anderson, Ben Mendelsohn, Henry Cavill, Andy Serkis, John Rhys-Davies, Mark Strong and Liam Cunningham among them, all recorded with full performance capture.[^c23]

The trailer shows more than cutscenes. IGN lists first-person sections with platforming and puzzles, ship customisation, off-duty activities, a hacking mini-game and narrative choices.[^c25]

## Why the date is where it is

The game was due this year. In August it moved to Q2 2027, and Chris Roberts was direct about the reason: he did not want it "launching into the attention buzz saw of GTA 6".[^c20]

## Why it matters

A store page is not a release, but it is the most checkable thing this project has produced in a long time. IGN counts 12 years of development.[^c21] PC Gamer notes that the August delay was met with scepticism, partly because these games have had so many dates before.[^c19] System requirements and an install size are commitments of a different kind from a stage announcement: they can be compared with what ships.[^c17]

The money gives the date its weight. Star Citizen passed $1 billion raised in May, has added a further $50 million since, and still has no release window of its own.[^c26] Squadron 42 is the part of the project with a quarter attached to it.[^c16]

## What we don't know

A day within the quarter, a price and any console plans are not given in the sources we read.[^c17] Whether the date holds is the open question.[^c19]
