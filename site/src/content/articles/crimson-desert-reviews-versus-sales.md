Crimson Desert sits at 77 on Metacritic and has sold 6 million copies.[^c5] A Pearl Abyss executive has now offered an explanation for the distance between those two numbers, and at least one critic has answered back.

## The argument

Lybee Park, chief executive of Pearl Abyss Europe, made two points to The Game Business. The first is about the build: "They had to play the build that is not the exact same version as the launch game."[^c3] The second is about time and habit. Reviewers, she said, tend to follow the main quest, while "our unique charm is spread all over our in-game world".[^c4]

## The facts around it

| Point | What the reporting says |
|---|---|
| Critical reception | Praise for the open world and combat, complaints about the story[^c6] |
| Player reception | Top of the Steam charts[^c6] |
| Was the review build different? | The game got a day-one patch, then a large first-week update that overhauled systems including inventory limits reviewers had complained about[^c8] |
| Did critics lack time? | Some played more than 100 hours before publishing[^c7] |
| What is next | The Charting the Unknown DLC, due on 29 October[^c9] |

## The reply

Rock Paper Shotgun's writer grants part of the case: critics are often short of time. Then comes the obvious question of whose fault that is, since the developer decides when review copies go out.[^c10] The larger objection is about roles. A critic is there to weigh a game, and a middling score is not a verdict on the people who love it.[^c11]

Kotaku makes a related point from the other side: sales and quality do not have to line up, in games any more than in film.[^c12]

## Our read

Both halves of Park's argument are partly supported by the record and partly undercut by it. The build point is the stronger one: a first-week update that changes systems reviewers criticised means some reviews describe a game that no longer quite exists.[^c8] The time point is weaker when some reviewers logged more than 100 hours.[^c7]

What neither side disputes is the sequence: the game changed quickly after launch, and the score did not.[^c8][^c5]

## What we don't know

How many sales came before and after the early patches is not in these reports, so it cannot be said how much the fixes drove the total.[^c5]
