Apple's long-rumoured smart home hub reportedly has a name, HomeView, and is expected to appear at the company's Welcome home event on 13 October.[^c4][^c5]

*This article collects claims from a single origin: reporting by Bloomberg's Mark Gurman, as relayed by Engadget and 9to5Mac. Apple has announced none of it. We will update it after the event.*

## The reported line-up

| Device | What is reported | Reported availability |
|---|---|---|
| HomeView, countertop | Display above a speaker base, A18 chip; space gray, silver, starlight or pink[^c7][^c8] | Mid-November[^c11] |
| HomeView, wall-mounted | Square base; space gray or silver only[^c7][^c8] | Mid-November[^c11] |
| HomePod mini, second generation | S10 chip in place of the S5, Siri AI features, Wi-Fi 7, Thread and Matter radio; adds pink, green and blue[^c13][^c14] | 30 October[^c12] |
| Apple TV | A19 chip[^c12] | 30 October[^c12] |
| Apple Home Collection | Doorbells, thermostats and outdoor cameras made with LG; Schneider Electric for Europe[^c16] | Later this year at the earliest[^c16] |

## How the hub is said to work

The name is the clue. The report says View was chosen to signal a screen you look at and talk to, driven mainly by Siri, and not a tablet you pick up.[^c6] In keeping with that, the hub is said to run a limited set of Apple apps with no App Store.[^c9] 9to5Mac adds that third-party widgets might be projected from a nearby iPhone.[^c10]

## Our read

If the report is right, the hub is being positioned as a fixture of a room and not as another computer, and the missing App Store is the choice that makes that clear.[^c9] It also means the product leans heavily on Siri being good enough to be the main way in.[^c6]

The staggered dates are worth noticing as well: the HomePod mini and Apple TV are reported for 30 October, with the hub itself weeks behind.[^c12][^c11]

## What we don't know

Prices are missing for everything. For the HomePod mini, 9to5Mac can only note that the current model rose from $99 to $129 in June.[^c15] Screen size, regional availability and how much of the Siri AI feature set arrives on day one are not in the reports we read.[^c13]
