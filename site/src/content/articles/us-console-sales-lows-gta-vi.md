The analyst who tracks US game sales for Circana has put the state of the console business in one line: "The US hardware market has not been in a more precarious position since the early 80s".[^c8] The numbers behind it are stark, and the biggest game of the year arrives in the middle of them.

## The numbers

| | Xbox | PlayStation |
|---|---|---|
| Unit sales, year to date, against last year | Down 33%[^c4] | Down 25%[^c4] |
| Where that leaves it | All-time low[^c6] | Lowest since 2013[^c6] |
| Average price paid for a new console in 2026 | $529[^c7] | $597[^c7] |
| Change in that price in a year | Up 26%[^c7] | Up 20%[^c7] |

The figures run to the end of August and come from Circana's retail tracking.[^c5] Both average prices are all-time highs for the US, and Mat Piscatella's summary was short: "Price sensitivity is becoming a real problem."[^c7]

## Why prices rose

The cause given is components. VGC reports that the increases are driven mostly by shortages caused by AI data centres.[^c9] Xbox chief Asha Sharma has described storage component costs more than doubling and then doubling again, with Xbox expecting to pay over five times the price of two years earlier by the 2027 holiday season.[^c13] Every current console has had its price raised, some more than once.[^c15]

## Our read: what GTA VI can and cannot do

Piscatella himself raised the obvious hope, saying the game might help ease some of the declines.[^c10] He attached two conditions, pricing and product availability, and those are the whole problem.[^c10]

Demand is not what is missing. Xbox's chief strategy officer said in June that "I can tell you definitively demand for our console exceeds the supply".[^c12] A retail buyer has warned that the game's release will likely lead to consoles selling out.[^c11] A game can make more people want a console; it cannot make more memory chips.[^c9]

So the likelier effect of a huge launch is a squeeze, with strong interest meeting high prices and thin stock, and not a simple recovery in unit sales.[^c11][^c7]

## What we don't know

Neither platform holder's own sales figures are in these reports; these are one tracker's US retail numbers.[^c5] The supply picture is not expected to change soon: Micron's chief executive has warned that memory shortages will last until at least 2028.[^c14]
