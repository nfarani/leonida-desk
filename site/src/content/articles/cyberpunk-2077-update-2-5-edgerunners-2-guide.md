Cyberpunk 2077 has a new free update. Update 2.5, the Edgerunners 2 Update, adds dual wielding, cross-platform mod support and a quest tied to the anime's second season, which premiered on Netflix on 20 October.[^c11][^c12] It launched on 22 October on PC, Mac, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with Switch 2 and the Mac App Store following in the days after.[^c22]

## The Edgerunners 2 quest

The tie-in starts as a gig from Wakako. V meets Weak Kingsley, one of the main characters of Edgerunners 2, during a job in a new location, Night City Prison.[^c13] The quest is a prequel to the series, so playing it first gives Episode 1 more context.[^c13]

It comes with Weak's full outfit, his revolver Coronation, and a new radio station, HKGN 102.FM, which plays music from both Edgerunners seasons.[^c14]

## What else is in 2.5

| Addition | What it does | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Dual wielding | A pistol or revolver in each hand, through the Double Trouble perk | Xbox Wire, The Verge[^c15][^c23] |
| Mod support | Browse and install supported mods in game, through mod.io | Xbox Wire[^c17] |
| Glamdocs | 40 cyberware styles from vendors at five locations | Xbox Wire[^c16] |
| Looks | 20 hairstyles and several clothing sets | Xbox Wire[^c16] |
| Exploration | 12 minor arcana tarot cards; Night City Tours narrated by Richard Night | Xbox Wire[^c18] |
| Quality of life | Stash filters; a save library for each V | Xbox Wire[^c18] |
| Settings | DLSS 4.5, glasses-free 3D effects, a field of view slider on console | The Verge[^c21] |
| Xbox Play Anywhere | One digital purchase covers Xbox Series X|S and PC | Xbox Wire[^c19] |

The developers rank dual wielding as one of the most significant changes to combat since update 2.0.[^c15]

## Mods on consoles

This is the change with the longest reach. According to CD Projekt Red's patch notes, mod support works on PC, Mac, PS5, PS5 Pro, Xbox Series X|S and Switch 2, and more mods will become available over time.[^c20] It is curated: players pick from supported mods in a Mods panel in the game, rather than installing anything they like.[^c17]

## How it surfaced

The update leaked before CD Projekt Red's reveal. A content creator published a video detailing it, then made it private, but fans had already shared the details.[^c24] The leak got the headline features right: the Double Trouble perk in the Cool skill tree, and two side quests.[^c25]
